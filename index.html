How to install

Install on Windows (about 3 minutes)

  1. Click Download for Windows above. If your browser warns that the file is not commonly downloaded, choose Keep (in Chrome, open the downloads list at the top right).
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click DocSigner-Setup.exe. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This only appears because the program is new.
  3. Click Next, then Install, then Finish. DocSigner opens and a shortcut appears on your Desktop.
Windows says “An Application Control policy has blocked this file”? Smart App Control is on. Ask IT, or see For IT: turn off Smart App Control below.

The first time you open it

  1. A blue bar asks for your pictures: click Choose pictures, then Choose… beside the picture in the Signature and Initials sections. No pictures yet? Tools > Prepare signature image turns a photo of your signature into a clean picture.
  2. Open a document with File > Open, drag a PDF or Word file onto the window, or right-click the file and choose Open with > DocSigner.
  3. Initials go on every page. Double-click anywhere on a page to place your signature.
  4. Press Ctrl+S, check the Signing Summary and click Save Signed PDF. The signed copy goes to Downloads as Name_SIGNED.pdf, opens, and is copied: in your email click Reply and press Ctrl+V to attach it. The original is never changed.

Good to know

  • DocSigner updates itself when you open it, then shows what is new. Help > Check for updates checks at any time.
  • If DocSigner closes before you save, it offers your signatures back next time.
  • To remove it: Help > Uninstall DocSigner.
How to use DocSigner

Sign a document

  1. Open it with File > Open (the folder icon), drag a PDF or Word file onto the window, or right-click the file and choose Open with > DocSigner.
  2. Initials go on its pages (by default not blank, cover or contents pages, or pages you sign). The Initials button (blue when on) leaves them off this document. Anything on printed words or a line turns amber: drag it clear.
  3. Numbered green dashed boxes mark likely signature spots: click one to sign there, or its cross to dismiss it. Tools > Find signature spots (Ctrl+F) brings them back.
  4. The date sits beside each signature and moves with it. Right-click the page to switch dates or initials on or off for this document.
  5. Alt + double-click to type text; Enter finishes. Click it again to edit, drag its corner to resize, right-click for font and size.
  6. Ctrl+S shows the Signing Summary (what is signed, and anything to check), then saves a signed copy to Downloads, opens it and copies it; the original never changes.

The little buttons on a selected box

  • Green tick: keep it here. On a box with a warning, this accepts the warning.
  • Red cross: remove it. On a green suggestion box, this dismisses it.
  • Blue pages: use this position on every page (initials and dates).
  • Blue calendar: add a date to this signature.

Page thumbnails and keys

  • Thumbnails: drag a marker (S, I, D, T, P) to move its box; double-click to sign; click a green number to go to that spot.
  • Double-click: signature. Shift + double-click: with the date. Ctrl + double-click: initials. Alt + double-click: text. Right-click a page for the same choices, and Start again.
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo; Enter keeps a box, Delete removes it, arrows nudge; Ctrl+/ lists every shortcut.
More: email it, stamps, your details, Settings

Send the signed PDF by email

  1. Saving also copies the signed PDF. In your email click Reply, then press Ctrl+V: it goes in as an attachment (Outlook).
  2. Mail program that does not take it, such as Thunderbird or Betterbird? Drag it in from the grey strip at the top of DocSigner (Drag into email). OK closes the strip.
  3. File > Copy signed PDF (Ctrl+Shift+C) copies it again. To stop the copying, untick Copy it too in Settings > Output.

Your name, title and organisation

  1. Type them once in Settings > Your details.
  2. Right-click a page and choose Add name here, Add title here or Add organisation here, or point at the spot and press Ctrl+Shift+N, T or O.

Stamps

  1. Choose your organisation's stamp picture in Settings > Stamp, with its size, ink and rotation.
  2. Right-click a page and choose Place stamp here, or point at the spot and press Ctrl+Shift+S.
  3. Click it to select it. Drag the round arrow on its corner to turn it (it snaps straight), slide its Ink bar to let the page show through, and drag the opposite corner to resize it.
  4. A stamp may sit on printed text: it gets no amber warning.

Check, Settings and look

  1. File > Signing Summary (Ctrl+R): page by page, what is signed and anything to check. It also comes up before saving, which fuses everything into the page: Acrobat cannot pick it out.
  2. Settings (the cog): date format (pick one, or Custom as in Excel, e.g. dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm); Output: _SIGNED after or SIGNED_ before the name, the folder, and open or copy after saving.
  3. View > Appearance: Light, Dark, or As the Computer.
For IT: turn off Smart App Control

Windows 11 has a feature called Smart App Control. It blocks programs that do not carry a paid digital signature, and DocSigner does not. If the installer will not start and says “An Application Control policy has blocked this file”, this is the reason. Turning it off removes the block. On most Windows versions it cannot be turned back on without reinstalling Windows.

Is it on? Check first

  1. Open Windows Security > App & browser control > Smart App Control settings. On or Evaluation means it must be turned off. Off means you are done.
  2. Or check from a command window. The result 0x0 means Off, 0x1 On, 0x2 Evaluation:
reg query HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\CI\Policy /v VerifiedAndReputablePolicyState

Method 1: Settings (easiest, about a minute)

  1. Press the Windows key, type Windows Security, and open it.
  2. Click App & browser control, then Smart App Control settings.
  3. Choose Off and confirm. No restart is needed. Then run the DocSigner installer again.

Method 2: Command line (for IT, or many laptops)

  1. Right-click the Start button and choose Terminal (Admin) or Command Prompt (Admin). It must be an administrator window.
  2. Paste this as one line and press Enter. It should answer “The operation completed successfully.”
reg add HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\CI\Policy /v VerifiedAndReputablePolicyState /t REG_DWORD /d 0 /f
  1. Restart the laptop, then run the check command above. It should now show 0x0.

Install on Mac (about 2 minutes)

  1. Click Download for Mac above.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click DocSigner-Mac.pkg. macOS says it was “Not Opened”: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. In the installer click Continue, then Install, enter your Mac password, then Close. DocSigner is now in Applications: open it from there.
Needs an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 14 or later. DocSigner updates itself each time it opens.

The first time you open it

  1. A blue bar asks for your pictures: click Choose pictures, then Choose… beside the picture in the Signature and Initials sections. No pictures yet? Tools > Prepare signature image turns a photo of your signature into a clean picture.
  2. Open a document with File > Open, drag a PDF or Word file onto the window, or right-click the file and choose Open With > DocSigner.
  3. First Word file only: allow DocSigner to control Microsoft Word. When Word asks for file access, click Select, choose the folder the document is in (for example My Drive), then Grant Access.
  4. Double-click a page to sign; Shift + double-click adds the date, Cmd + double-click places initials, Option + double-click types text. Cmd+S shows the Signing Summary, then saves Name_SIGNED.pdf to Downloads, opens it and copies it: in your email click Reply and press Cmd+V to attach it. The original is never changed.

Good to know

  • DocSigner updates itself when you open it, usually in a second or two, then says what is new. DocSigner > Check for updates checks at any time.
  • If DocSigner closes before you save, it offers your signatures back next time.
  • To remove it: Help > Uninstall DocSigner moves it to the Bin. Your settings are kept.
